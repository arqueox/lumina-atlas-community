## Lumina Atlas Community 0.3.0

A native Google TV launcher in the Noir design. It replaces the 0.1.0 WebView client and needs no server.

- On first launch, **Ligar fonte** asks for your own IPTV source: an Xtream link, any M3U/M3U8 address, or a `.m3u` playlist or `.url` shortcut chosen on the TV. It is stored only on that device. The APK contains no link, account or token.
- Direto, Filmes and Séries with details, seasons and episodes. Player with FFmpeg audio decoders; ▲/▼ changes channel, ◀/▶ seeks, OK pauses.
- Launcher home with your apps, an optional *A ver agora* row (needs notification access) and an optional accessibility service for the Home button.

0.3.0 is signed with a new key: uninstall 0.1.0 before installing. It installs and starts on a Google TV Streamer 4K; playback with a range of providers has not been tested yet, so this is a pre-release.
